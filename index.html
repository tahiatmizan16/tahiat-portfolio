Iron & manganese removal

Cigarette butts are collected, washed, unwrapped and oven-dried, then carbonized in a muffle furnace at 500°C for 4 hours to make charcoal. The charcoal is then impregnated with KOH (carbonaceous material to KOH ratios of 1:1.5, 1:2 and 1:2.5) to produce activated carbon.

An overview of the laboratory work, from the filter columns to the water quality tests.

Removal efficiency (%) of each filter medium. The activated carbon filter made from cigarette filters removed 99% of iron and 97% of chloride, and performed best on most parameters.
